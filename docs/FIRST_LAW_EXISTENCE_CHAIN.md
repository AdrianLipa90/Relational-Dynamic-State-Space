## L1 — First Law of Existence

\[
\boxed{
L_1:\quad
\text{absolute nothingness is not a realizable state}
}
\]

Absolute nothingness contains no object, distinction or relation by which it
could be established as a state. The historical claim identifier A0 is retained
for repository compatibility, but its canonical kind is FOUNDATIONAL_LAW.

The empty set, algebraic zero, a zero vector, zero phase and relational zero
are mathematical objects and must never be identified with absolute
nothingness.

## Two different meanings of potential

The word potential occurs twice and the two objects must remain distinct.

First:

\[
\boxed{\mathcal P_E}
\]

is the primitive existence potential left after absolute nothingness is
excluded. It is modal/foundational and carries no physical magnitude, field
strength or force law.

Later:

\[
\boxed{\mathcal A_B}
\]

is a local phase-connection/potential representative associated with Berry
holonomy. It is gauge-dependent and belongs to the geometric phase layer.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\mathcal P_E\neq\mathcal A_B}.
\]

## 5. Imaginary generator from the two Hamlet involutions

Let \(B=\{-1,+1\}\) be the ordered Hamlet branch set and let

\[
V_B=\mathbb R e_-\oplus\mathbb R e_+
\]

be its free real linear span.

Two canonical involutions act on this span:

\[
S e_b=e_{-b}
\]

for branch swap and

\[
P e_b=b\,e_b
\]

for branch polarity.

They satisfy

\[
S^2=P^2=I
\]

and

\[
SP=-PS.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
J:=SP
}
\]

obeys

\[
J^2
=
SPSP
=
-S^2P^2
=
\boxed{-I}.
\]

So the algebraic role of the imaginary generator is not inserted by hand:

\[
\boxed{
i\;\widehat{=}\;J.
}
\]

Reversing the branch ordering sends \(J\mapsto-J\), which is the expected
complex-conjugation/orientation ambiguity rather than a new algebraic
structure.

\[

e^{(\phi_1+\phi_2)J},

\[
\boxed{

Thus the phase orbit is downstream of the two Hamlet involutions.

## 10. Canonical Hamlet loop -> Berry sign -> minimal spin one half

Choose either normalized branch pole \(\psi_0\). The canonical orbit generated

\[

The symmetry-fixed Hermitian form gives

\[
\langle\psi,\dot\psi\rangle=0,
\]

so this is a horizontal lift of its projective path. At the endpoint,

\[
\psi(\pi)=e^{\pi J}\psi_0=-\psi_0.
\]

The ray has therefore returned to itself while the horizontal lift has picked
up the phase

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Hol}_{\rm Berry}=-1.
}
\]

The projected Bloch-sphere path is the primitive great-circle loop and bounds
a hemisphere,

\[
\Omega=2\pi.
\]

\[
e^{is\Omega}
=
e^{i2\pi s}
=
-1.
\]

Hence

\[
s\in\mathbb Z+\frac12,

\[
\boxed{
s_{\min}=\frac12.
}
\]

The doubled traversal gives

\[
e^{i4\pi(1/2)}=+1.
\]

The \(-1\) sign is therefore no longer an independent premise; it is the
holonomy of the canonical horizontal Hamlet \(J\)-orbit.
