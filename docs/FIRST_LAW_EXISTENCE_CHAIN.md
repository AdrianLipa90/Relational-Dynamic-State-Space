## L1 — First Law of Existence

\[
\boxed{
L_1:\quad
\text{absolute nothingness is not a realizable state}
}
\]

Absolute nothingness contains no object, distinction or relation by which it
could be established as a state. The historical claim identifier A0 is retained
for repository compatibility, but its canonical kind is FOUNDATIONAL_LAW.

The empty set, algebraic zero, a zero vector, zero phase and relational zero
are mathematical objects and must never be identified with absolute
nothingness.

## Two different meanings of potential

The word potential occurs twice and the two objects must remain distinct.

First:

\[
\boxed{\mathcal P_E}
\]

is the primitive existence potential left after absolute nothingness is
excluded. It is modal/foundational and carries no physical magnitude, field
strength or force law.

Later:

\[
\boxed{\mathcal A_B}
\]

is a local phase-connection/potential representative associated with Berry
holonomy. It is gauge-dependent and belongs to the geometric phase layer.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\mathcal P_E\neq\mathcal A_B}.
\]

## 10. Hamlet Pauli/SU(2) half representation and Berry closure

The same Hamlet operators give the Pauli triple

\[
\boxed{
\sigma_x=S,
\qquad
\sigma_z=-P,
\qquad
\sigma_y=-iJ.
}
\]

They satisfy

\[
\sigma_k^2=I,
\qquad
[\sigma_i,\sigma_j]
=
2i\epsilon_{ijk}\sigma_k.
\]

With

\[
T_k=\frac{\sigma_k}{2},
\]

we obtain

\[
\boxed{
[T_i,T_j]
=
i\epsilon_{ijk}T_k,
}
\]

the defining two-dimensional representation of \(\mathfrak{su}(2)\).

The quadratic Casimir is

\[
\sum_k T_k^2
=
\frac34 I.
\]

For the standard non-negative representation label \(j\),

\[
j(j+1)=\frac34,
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{
j=\frac12.
}
\]

This fixes the half representation **before** Berry matching.

Exponentiating a normalized axis gives

\[
U(\theta)
=
\exp\left(
-\frac{i\theta}{2}
\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma
\right),
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
U(2\pi)=-I,
\qquad
U(4\pi)=+I.
}
\]

### Independent Berry/Euler closure

In the right-\(\mathbb C_J\) module chart, the normalized balanced Hamlet
state is

\[
\Psi_0
=
\frac{I+S}{\sqrt2}
\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad

\[

\[

\[

\]

so

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Hol}_{\rm Berry}=-1.
}
\]

Thus the Berry sign agrees with the \(SU(2)\) spinor sign at \(2\pi\), while
Euler's

\[
e^{i\pi}=-1
\]

is the common scalar closure. The doubled traversal returns to \(+1\).

The fixed unitary chart

\[

\begin{pmatrix}

maps the operator orbit to

\[

The physical identification of the mathematical \(j=\tfrac12\)
