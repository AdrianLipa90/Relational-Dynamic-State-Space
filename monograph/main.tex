\begin{abstract}
This monograph defines a general relational tensor--scalar, orbital--fractal
algebra. The construction is deliberately prior to any specific spacetime,
metric, or physical interpretation. Its primitives are relational
admissibility, a minimal point carrier, projective binary distinction,
tensor--scalar grading, typed relations, semantic scales, orbital actions,
relative Moir\'e transformations, recursive refinement, and a Zero-Axis phase
sector. Quantities such as orbit, winding, associator, commutator, and
refinement residual are derived rather than stored as independent state
coordinates. No Planck scale, Minkowski structure, gravitational
interpretation, associativity, commutativity, or exact self-similarity is
assumed by the general algebra.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope}

The object developed here is not a spacetime model. It is a general algebra
of relations. Concrete geometric and physical constructions may later be
representations of this algebra, but they are not part of its definition.

The typed algebraic family is written
\[
\boxed{
\Arel=\left\{\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p,q}\right\},
}
\]
where \((p,q)\) is tensor rank, \(n\in\mathbb N_0\) is refinement level,
and \(\sigma\in\Sigma\) is a semantic scale or context.

The scalar sector is not a separate ontology. It is the tensor rank-zero
sector:
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb K_{\sigma,n}=\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{0,0}.
}
\]

\section{Relational admissibility}

The foundational rule is stated as an admissibility condition:
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Adm}(x)
\Longrightarrow
\exists\,y,r:\operatorname{Rel}_{r}(x,y).
}
\tag{RA0}
\]
An admissible observable is therefore not defined as an isolated object.
The statement is intentionally weaker and cleaner than a physical claim: it
does not assign a spacetime, energy, length, or dynamics.

A primitive relation is typed as a binary arrow
\[
\boxed{r:a\to b.}
\]
This is a definition of the primitive relational signature, not a theorem of
RA0. Higher-arity relations may be constructed separately from binary
relations or introduced by an explicit extension of the signature.

The absence of an admitted relation is not identified with algebraic zero.
For every typed sector there may be a zero element
\[
0_{\sigma,n}^{p,q}\in\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p,q},
\]
while ``no relation'' is a separate condition.

\section{Minimal carrier and minimal distinction}

The minimal carrier of the general algebra is a point
\[
\boxed{p.}
\]
No Planck length or other physical scale is attached to it at this level.
A later physical representation may provide such a scale through a separate
bridge.

The reference minimal two-channel realization of a distinction uses a
non-zero vector in \(\mathbb C^2\), modulo global phase:
\[
|\psi\rangle
=\alpha|0\rangle+\beta|1\rangle,
\qquad
|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1,
\]
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb P(\mathbb C^2)=\CP\simeq S^2.
}
\tag{REF-CP1}
\]
The natural projective metric is Fubini--Study:
\[
\boxed{
d_{\FS}([\psi],[\phi])
=\arccos|\langle\psi|\phi\rangle|.
}
\]

A relation \(r\) may select two projective states
\[
r\longmapsto(p_r^-,p_r^+)\in\CP\times\CP.
\]
Maximal distinction is the special case
\[
\langle p_r^-|p_r^+\rangle=0,
\qquad
d_{\FS}=\frac{\pi}{2}.
\]
Generic relations need not be maximal and therefore need not select
antipodal Bloch points. Equation (REF-CP1) is a reference realization of a
complex binary distinction. It is not claimed to follow from RA0 alone.

\section{Recursive relational refinement}

A selected relational point may itself become the carrier of another
minimal distinction. Let
\[
V_n=\{-1,+1\}^{n}.
\]
For \(\omega\in V_n\), define the binary refinement
\[
\boxed{
p_\omega\longmapsto
\{p_{\omega-},p_{\omega+}\}.
}
\tag{RR1}
\]
RR1 is an explicit second foundational axiom: RA0 requires relation but does
not by itself force recursive refinement. Thus the general construction contains a recursive relational tower
\[
p\to\{p_-,p_+\}
\to\{p_{--},p_{-+},p_{+-},p_{++}\}
\to\cdots.
\]

Fractality means compatibility across refinement levels, not a requirement of
perfect geometric self-similarity. If
\[
F:\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p,q}
\to
\mathcal A_{S(\sigma),n+1}^{p,q}
\]
is a refinement map and \(\star\) a relational product, define
\[
\boxed{
\Delta_F(X,Y)
=F(X\star Y)-F(X)\star F(Y).
}
\]
Exact self-similarity is the special case \(\Delta_F=0\). Non-zero residuals
remain legitimate algebraic information.

\section{Tensor--scalar grading and typed relations}

Each typed sector carries an additive module structure over a scalar domain \(\mathbb K\):
\[
\boxed{
(\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p,q},+,0,-,\mathbb K\curvearrowright\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p,q}).
}
\]
The abstract algebra does not fix \(\mathbb K=\mathbb C\); the reference kernel uses complex components. This additive structure is required before expressions such as commutators, associators, and refinement residuals can be formed as differences.

Elements are typed by tensor rank, semantic scale, and refinement level:
\[
X\in\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p,q}.
\]
Tensor product and contraction have the schematic types
\[
\otimes:
\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p,q}\times
\mathcal A_{\tau,m}^{r,s}
\to
\mathcal A_{\upsilon,k}^{p+r,q+s},
\]
\[
\operatorname{Contr}:
\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p,q}
\to
\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p-1,q-1},
\]
whenever the relevant type and relation constraints are satisfied.

A typed relation is written
\[
r:a\to b
\]
and may induce transport
\[
\boxed{
T_r:\mathcal A_a\to\mathcal A_b.
}
\tag{DEF-T}
\]
No universal transport law is imposed before the relation type is supplied.

\section{Relational product: no premature associativity}

Let
\[
\star_R:
\mathcal A\times\mathcal A\to\mathcal A
\]
denote a relational product in a compatible typed sector. The current
foundation does not assume either commutativity or associativity.

Their failures are measured, not hidden:
\[
\boxed{
[X,Y]_{\star}
=X\star Y-Y\star X,
}
\]
\[
\boxed{
\alpha(X,Y,Z)
=(X\star Y)\star Z-X\star(Y\star Z).
}
\]
Commutative or associative subclasses are recovered when the corresponding
residual vanishes.

\section{Semantic scales}

Semantic scale is not assumed to be a single real parameter. Let
\[
\boxed{\sigma\in\Sigma}
\]
where \(\Sigma\) is a typed partially ordered or categorical scale
structure. A coarse-graining morphism is written
\[
S_{\sigma\to\tau}:
\mathcal A_{\sigma,n}^{p,q}
\to
\mathcal A_{\tau,n}^{p,q}.
\]
Such a map is admissible only when the scale structure contains the required
relation. This prevents unrelated semantic classes from being collapsed by
arithmetic alone.

A common semantic quotient of two types exists only when there is a common
admissible upper scale. Thus semantic compatibility is part of the algebraic
type system rather than an after-the-fact annotation.

\section{Orbital action and relative Moir\'e structure}

Let \(G\) act on admissible algebra elements through
\[
U_g:\mathcal A\to\mathcal A.
\]
An orbit is derived:
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Orb}(X)=\{U_gX:g\in G\}.
}
\]
It is not an independent coordinate carried by \(X\).

Moir\'e structure is intrinsically relational. For two local orbital actions,
\[
\boxed{
M_{ij}=U_iU_j^{-1}.
}
\tag{DER-M}
\]
In a scalar-angle realization this reduces to the wrapped relative angle
\[
\Delta\theta_{ij}
=\operatorname{wrap}_{2\pi}(\theta_i-\theta_j).
\]

Semantic scaling and Moir\'e action need not commute. The mixed residual
\[
\boxed{
[M,S]=MS-SM
}
\]
is therefore a legitimate observable of the algebra rather than an error.

\section{Zero Axis and the phase realization}

The companion Zero Axis model supplies the projective coordinate
\[
\boxed{
\Omega(s)=\frac{s}{1-s}.
}
\]
On the critical axis
\[
\Zaxis=\left\{s\in\mathbb C:\Re s=\frac12\right\},
\]
one has the exact equivalence
\[
\boxed{
\Re s=\frac12
\Longleftrightarrow
|\Omega(s)|=1.
}
\tag{ZA-BRIDGE}
\]
Hence the compactified Zero Axis has a natural unit-circle realization. This
identity is imported from the companion Zero Axis model as a bridge and is
not an axiom of the general relational algebra. The
\(U(1)\) phase sector is therefore treated here as a projective realization
of \(\Zaxis\), not as an unrelated extra primitive.

For a closed phase path \(\gamma\), winding is derived:
\[
\boxed{
W(\gamma)
=\frac{1}{2\pi i}
\oint_{\Omega\circ\gamma}u^{-1}\,du.
}
\]
Likewise the phase residual is derived from accumulated holonomy. Neither
winding nor residual is stored as an independent coordinate of the base
algebra element.

\section{One-axiom closure candidate}

The current canonical foundation uses two explicit axioms,
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RA0}+\mathrm{RR1}.}
\]
The finite independence witnesses show that the original RA0 is too weak to
force recursive refinement:
\[
\boxed{\mathrm{RA0}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{RR1}.}
\]

A stronger candidate foundational statement is therefore introduced:
\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{RA0}^{*}:
\text{every admitted carrier hosts an admitted non-degenerate binary distinction}
}
\]
together with the closure clause that the two selected endpoint occurrences
are themselves admitted carriers.

Let a relational occurrence be indexed by
\[
\omega\in\{-1,+1\}^{n}.
\]
If \(p_{\omega}\) is admitted, RA0* supplies
\[
D(p_{\omega})
=
\bigl(
p_{\omega-},
p_{\omega+}
\bigr),
\]
and both endpoints are admitted. By induction on \(n\), for every finite
depth \(N\) the complete binary address set
\[
\boxed{
V_n=\{-1,+1\}^{n},
\qquad
0\le n\le N,
}
\]
is generated. Hence
\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{RA0}^{*}\Rightarrow\mathrm{RR1}
}
\]
in the path-labelled occurrence semantics.

This is a genuine strengthening of RA0, not a proof from its old wording.
RA0* explicitly adds binary non-degeneracy and endpoint admissibility. Those
clauses are precisely what makes the induction valid.

The underlying carrier presentation may nevertheless be cyclic. For example,
\[
D(a)=(a,b),
\qquad
D(b)=(a,b)
\]
satisfies RA0* while reusing the carrier labels \(a,b\). Its unfolding is
still a complete binary tree of distinct path-labelled occurrences. Thus
recursive relational structure does not by itself imply ontically fresh
carrier identities:
\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{RA0}^{*}\not\Rightarrow\mathrm{NOVELTY}.
}
\]
If fresh carrier identity at every refinement level is required, that must be
introduced by an additional axiom or proved from additional structure.

Accordingly RA0* is recorded as a candidate axiom and
\(\mathrm{RR1}\) as a theorem candidate under RA0*. The canonical
two-axiom status is retained until this stronger wording is explicitly
promoted.

\section{Explicit non-assumptions}

The general algebra does not assume:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a Planck-scale minimal point;
  \item a particular spacetime, metric, or gravitational law;
  \item associativity or commutativity of \(\star_R\);
  \item exact preservation of products under refinement;
  \item a one-dimensional numerical semantic scale;
  \item winding, orbit, or residual as independent state coordinates;
  \item a physical interpretation of an algebraic structure without a
        separate bridge theorem.
\end{itemize}
